In November 2025, the following news and developments have been highlighted for major carbon accounting software providers:

Watershed:
- Released Q3 2025 product updates including AI-accelerated reporting features designed to enhance data accuracy and reporting speed.
- Announced new capabilities for EU CSRD reporting with a dedicated software solution supporting over 1,100 ESG data points and integration with frameworks like CDP and TCFD.
- Continues to offer extensive enterprise integrations and tools for actionable decarbonization planning and supplier engagement.[1][2][3]

Persefoni:
- Recently secured $23 million in funding aimed at expanding AI functionalities, such as smart emission factor matching and a Product Carbon Footprint/LCA capability.
- Focused on building audit-ready disclosures aligned with global standards including CSRD, ISSB, and CDP.
- Plans to introduce a self-serve analytics builder and new AI features by late 2025 to bolster product offerings towards profitability.[4]

Greenly:
- Launched a new AI-powered platform automating complex carbon data tasks, including Scope 3 emissions modeling through supplier commitments.
- Provides interactive scenario visualization tools and reports compliant with multiple sustainability standards including CSRD and IFRS.
- Operates on energy-efficient infrastructure such as Google Cloud’s green-data centers, enhancing sustainability of its own operations.[5]

carbmee:
- Secured €20 million funding to develop its AI-driven carbon management software focused on value-chain-wide emissions data integration and granularity, especially for Scope 3.
- Offers centralized carbon data management, enabling corporate-level, product-level, supplier, and SKU tracking similar to financial data management in ERP systems.[6]

osapiens:
- Showcased the HUB Reporting Cockpit, a unified interface for automated collection and management of sustainability KPIs aligned with reporting standards like VSME, GRI, SASB, CSRD, and Corporate Carbon Footprint.
- Recently introduced two new modules to enhance its sustainability data management capabilities.[7]

Sweep:
- Continues to emphasize automated sustainable data collection across carbon emissions, water, energy, and waste metrics with AI-powered tools.
- Has enabled clients like Margo and SSE to quickly scale scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions tracking and transition from manual to continuous data collection for improved compliance and decision-making.[8]

Normative:
- While no new specific November 2025 updates surfaced, Normative remains focused on scientific rigor in carbon calculations with extensive emission factor databases and client advisory services.

Industry Trends:
- Strong momentum in AI integration to automate and refine carbon accounting and reporting processes.
- Growing focus on compliance with evolving global ESG regulations, notably the EU CSRD.
- Increasing enterprise adoption of platforms that provide comprehensive, audit-ready disclosures and supply chain emissions visibility.
- Expansion of scenario modeling and actionable decarbonization tools across major platforms.

These insights reflect a competitive and fast-evolving carbon accounting software market with ongoing product innovations, strategic funding, and enterprise partnerships primarily aimed at enabling companies to meet stringent sustainability targets and regulatory requirements.[2][3][1][4][5][6][7][8]
