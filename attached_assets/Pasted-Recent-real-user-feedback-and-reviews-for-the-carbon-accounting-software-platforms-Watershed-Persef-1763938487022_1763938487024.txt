Recent real user feedback and reviews for the carbon accounting software platforms Watershed, Persefoni, Greenly, Carbmee, Osapiens, and Sweep as of November 2025 are as follows:

### Watershed
- Pros: Recognized for comprehensive emissions management including audit-grade data, automated data collection, and strong supply chain (Scope 3) emissions tracking. It provides real-time insights aiding fast, data-driven sustainability decisions.
- Cons: Some users report that integrating Watershed can be complex and resource-intensive, particularly for organizations without established sustainability programs.[1][2]

### Persefoni
- Pros: Highly regarded for accuracy, transparency, and an advanced calculation engine aligned with standards like GHGP and PCAF. Artificial intelligence helps detect anomalies and supports SEC-compliant workflows with XBRL tagging for registrants. It is favored by enterprises and asset managers for standardized carbon accounting across portfolios.
- Cons: Some users note the interface is not always intuitive, with navigation challenges, and the pricing may be steep for smaller organizations.
- Clients include large names like Xerox, Aramark, and The New York Times.
- Its AI-assisted workflows and real-time collaboration tools make it especially effective for complex, enterprise needs.[3][4][5][2][1]

### Greenly
- Pros: Users appreciate its real-time and precise carbon accounting, customizable reports, and user-friendly interface. It is particularly valued by smaller to medium companies looking for a personalized approach and support in reducing environmental impact.
- Cons: Some feedback points to the tool being expensive for budget-limited organizations.
- The software is praised for ease of implementation, ongoing feature updates, and strong expert support during customer journeys.[6][7]

### Carbmee
- Carbmee emphasizes empowering enterprises with informed decision-making and impactful carbon reduction strategies through its EIS™ platform. User reviews and detailed usability information are less prominent but positioning suggests a strong fit for enterprise-scale carbon management.[8]

### Sweep, Osapiens
- These platforms were less covered in recent user reviews from the searched sources. However, Sweep was noted as among the top carbon accounting platforms in some comparative listings.[9]
