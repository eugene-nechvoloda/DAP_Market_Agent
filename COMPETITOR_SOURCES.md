## Time Filter Definitions

- **7days**: Last 7 days
- **1month**: Last 30 days
- **current_month**: All content from the current calendar month
- **quarter**: Last 3 months

## Category Types

- **news**: Company news and announcements
- **case_studies**: Customer success stories and case studies
- **analyst_reports**: Third-party analyst reports and research
- **changelog**: Product changelog and release notes
- **g2_reviews**: User reviews from G2
- **gartner_reviews**: User reviews from Gartner
- **gartner_likes_dislikes**: User feedback on Gartner
- **product_updates**: Product feature updates and releases
